# Happyland section on the homepage

The place portrait follows “How it works” at `/#happyland`. Its factual basis is Father Severin's account of Happyland in Tondo, Manila. Visitor copy lives under `happyland.*` in `src/lib/messages.ts` and is available in English, German, Spanish and Filipino. `HappylandSection` renders four photographs from the original 21.gifts Happyland page and three short observations. It makes no claim that a gift on 21.gifts reaches someone in Happyland or that anyone pictured receives funds.

The live `https://21.gifts/` homepage currently serves all eight earlier Happyland photographs. At JK's request, this local redesign reuses four of those existing project files: `main-street.webp`, `food-stall.webp`, `home.webp`, and `household.webp`. The food-stall scene leads; the other three appear in equal 4:3 frames so portrait photographs do not create uneven rows. The other four images remain out of this section. The Word file and removed images stay available in Git history.

The original editorial document credits Father Severin for `main-street.webp`; the photographer of the other three pictures is not named there. Its approval, image-rights, and consent fields remain unfilled, including for identifiable children in `home.webp`. Existing publication does not establish a reuse license or consent. Before this local version is published, obtain and document the photographer permissions and relevant subject/guardian consents.

Update all four catalogs when editing the portrait. Keep claims tied to the source account, avoid invented recipient stories, and review the rendered mobile and desktop layouts.
