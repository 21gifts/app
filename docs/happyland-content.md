# Happyland section on the homepage

The place portrait follows “Why” at `/{locale}#happyland`; a discovery card after “How it works” links to it. Its factual basis is Father Severin's account of Happyland in Tondo, Manila. Visitor copy lives under `happyland.*` in `src/lib/messages.ts` and is available in English, German, Spanish and Filipino. `HappylandSection` renders four photographs from the original 21.gifts Happyland page and three short observations. It makes no claim that a donation on 21.gifts reaches someone in Happyland or that anyone pictured receives funds.

The live `https://21.gifts/` homepage currently serves all eight earlier Happyland photographs. This redesign shows four of those existing project files: `main-street.webp`, `food-stall.webp`, `home.webp`, and `household.webp`. The food-stall scene leads; the other three appear in equal 4:3 frames so portrait photographs do not create uneven rows. The other four photographs (`from-above.webp`, `lane.webp`, `neighbourhood.webp`, and `waste.webp`) stay in `public/happyland/`, and the English editorial document stays at [Happyland_English.docx](happyland/Happyland_English.docx). This section does not show them.

The original editorial document credits Father Severin for `main-street.webp`; the photographer of the other three pictures is not named there. Its approval, image-rights, and consent fields remain unfilled, including for identifiable children in `home.webp`. Existing publication does not establish a reuse license or consent. Before this redesign is published, obtain and document the photographer permissions and relevant subject/guardian consents.

Update all four catalogs when editing the portrait. Keep claims tied to the source account, avoid invented recipient stories, and review the rendered mobile and desktop layouts.
